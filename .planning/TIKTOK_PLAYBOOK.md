## PART 1: ALGORITHM FOUNDATION

### The 2026 TikTok Algorithm (Core Weights)

**Algorithm Signal Hierarchy:**
- **Watch Time / Completion Rate** (40-50% weight) — Single most important metric
- **Shares** (High) — Outweighs likes 10x over
- **Saves** (High) — Indicates lasting value
- **Comments** (Medium) — Especially 5+ word substantive replies
- **Replays / Loop Rate** (Very High) — Viewers rewatching indicates quality
- **Profile Visits** (Medium) — Interest signal

**Likes are the weakest signal.** Stop optimizing for them.

### The 3-Phase Distribution Model

1. **Initial Test Pool (200-500 views)** → Algorithm measures your hook, completion rate, early shares
2. **Expanded Pool (1K-50K views)** → If test metrics hit 70%+ completion, video moves here
3. **Viral Breakout (100K+ views)** → Strong performers get exponential push

**Key Insight:** Every video is judged independently. Follower count barely matters at test stage. New accounts with zero followers can go viral if the hook and completion rate are strong.

### The 70% Completion Rate Threshold

Videos that hit **70%+ completion rate** receive strong distribution expansion. Below 50%, your video dies algorithmically.

**Completion Rate Targets by Video Length:**
- **Under 15 seconds:** 75%+ (exceptional)
- **15-30 seconds:** 60%+ (good), 75%+ (exceptional)
- **30-60 seconds:** 45%+ (good), 60%+ (exceptional)
- **60+ seconds:** 30%+ (good), 45%+ (exceptional)

**Strategy:** Aim for 20-45 second videos for dAIly. Tight pacing = higher completion = faster distribution.

### The First 3 Seconds Are Do-Or-Die

TikTok decides in 0.5-1.5 seconds whether to keep the viewer. Every video lives or dies on the hook.

**Never open with:**
- "Hey guys..."
- Slow intros
- Logos or branding first
- Talking heads (faceless accounts especially)
- Anything generic

**Always open with:**
- Movement, text, or sudden visual change
- Curiosity gap or promise
- Specific problem or outcome
- A search phrase from the first word

## PART 2: POSTING STRATEGY & CADENCE

### Optimal Posting Frequency

**For dAIly Account (Pre-Launch Growth Phase):**
- **Target:** 3-5 videos per week (consistency over volume)
- **Days:** Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun (stagger to keep algorithm fresh)
- **Times:** Post at consistent times (algorithm learns your schedule and allocates capacity)
  - Ideal windows: 9-10am (commute), 12-1pm (lunch), 6-7pm (evening wind-down)
  - Test 3 different times for 2 weeks, track completion rates, pick winner
- **Avoid:** Daily posting unless you can sustain quality (diminishing returns after 5x/week for new accounts)

**Why:** 4-7 posts/week at consistent times outperforms sporadic high-volume. Algorithm learns to predict your audience's engagement windows.

### Initial Growth Phase (Months 1-3)

**Goal:** 500-1,000 followers (test audience)

- Post every other day (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun) = 4x/week
- Hook test: Create 3-5 different hook variations for the same core message, post at different times
- Track completion rates obsessively (not views, not likes)
- By week 3-4: Double down on the hook format that hits 70%+ completion

**Months 2-3:** You now have data. Keep the winning format at 60% of your output, experiment with 40%.

### Optimal Timing by Content Type

| Content Type | Best Post Time | Why |
|--------------|----------------|-----|
| Morning routine tips | 6-8am | Viewers planning their day |
| Email/calendar chaos | 8-10am | Relatable during work start |
| AI productivity hacks | 12-1pm | Lunch scroll |
| Executive briefing benefits | 5-7pm | End-of-day wind-down |
| Motivational/aspirational | 9-10pm | Before bed reflection |

**Test a time slot for 7 days, measure completion rate, lock it in.**

## PART 7: COMPLETION RATE OPTIMIZATION (HITTING 70%+)

### Why Videos Lose Viewers

**Most common drop-off points:**
- 0-3 seconds: Hook fails. Viewer swipes immediately.
- 5-8 seconds: Pacing slows. Setup drags.
- 12-18 seconds: Promise hasn't been delivered. Viewer leaves before payoff.

### Tactics to Hit 70%+ Completion

#### 1. **Hook-to-Value Velocity**
- First 3 seconds: Hook (curiosity or promise)
- Second 3 seconds: Context or small value hit
- By 8 seconds: Viewer should feel "OK, this is worth watching"

**Test:** Post your video. Check TikTok Analytics after 24 hours. If completion rate drops from 80% (0-3s) to 50% (3-8s), your hook promises something the content doesn't deliver fast enough.

#### 2. **Visual Rhythm & Scene Changes**
- Change visuals every 3-5 seconds
- Use zoom, pan, text animation, color shift
- No static shots longer than 5 seconds in the first 30 seconds

**Why:** Faceless content has no personality to hold attention. Movement does the work.

#### 3. **Pacing with On-Screen Text**
- Viewers read before listening. On-screen text forces them to stay.
- Use bold, large, readable text (minimum 48pt)
- Text should preview the voiceover by 1-2 seconds

**Why:** Creates anticipation. Viewer wants to see the visual payoff.

#### 4. **Audio Pacing**
- No long pauses
- Voiceover should match editing rhythm (fast cuts = fast talking, slow sections = longer holds)
- Use subtle background music to maintain momentum

#### 5. **The "Delayed Reveal" Structure**
- 0-5s: Pose a problem or question
- 5-15s: Tease the answer, don't give it yet ("Here's what most people don't know...")
- 15-20s: Deliver the answer (payoff)
- 20-25s: Show how to use it or social proof
- 25-30s: CTA (link in bio)

**Completion Rate:** 75%+ (curiosity gap naturally extends watch time)

#### 6. **Loop-Friendly Endings**
- End with a visual or audio that makes viewers want to rewatch
- "Wait, I missed something" moments are gold
- Last 2 seconds should loop back to first 2 seconds (optional, advanced)

**Example:** Last frame is a question ("Which one would you choose?") that mirrors the hook.

#### 7. **The "Progress Bar" Illusion**
- Use on-screen text to show progression (e.g., "3 of 5 tips...2 of 5 tips...")
- Viewers stick around because they're tracking progress
- Completion rate: 70%+ (completion psychology)

## PART 9: AI CONTENT COMPLIANCE

### TikTok's AI Labeling Rules (2025-2026)

**What Requires the "AI-Generated" Label:**
- AI voiceovers (fully synthetic voices)
- AI-generated visuals of people (deepfakes, synthetic avatars)
- Significantly altered video using AI (face swaps, synthetic backgrounds showing real locations)

**What Does NOT Require a Label:**
- Auto-captions
- AI-written scripts
- Text overlays
- Color correction
- Background removal
- Subtitle generation
- Motion graphics (unless they synthesize real people)

**For dAIly Content (Mostly Faceless):**
- **Screen recordings** of dAIly UI: No label needed
- **Motion graphics + voiceover** about dAIly: Label only if the voiceover is AI-synthesized and realistic
- **Ambient footage + text overlays**: No label needed
- **AI-generated background animations**: No label needed (not depicting real people)

### Labeling Process (Takes 3 Seconds)

During posting:
1. Tap "More options ..."
2. Toggle "AI-generated content"
3. Confirm and publish

**Important:** Labeled AI content does NOT get penalized algorithmically. The label appears as "creator labeled as AI-generated." Reach is not suppressed.

**Penalties (for undisclosed AI content):**
- 1st offense: Content removal + strike
- 2nd: 7-day posting restriction
- 3rd: 30-day restriction
- 4th: Permanent monetization ban
- 5th: Account termination

**Safe Rule:** When in doubt, label. There's no penalty for labeling content that didn't strictly require it.

### Recommended Approach for dAIly

- Use **human voiceover** or **Eleven Labs realistic voice** (label as AI if using Eleven Labs)
- Use **authentic testimonials** from beta testers (no deepfakes)
- Use **screen recordings** of actual dAIly interface (no label needed)
- Use **Synthesia for motion graphics + voiceover** (label as required)
- **Disclose transparently:** "Built with AI video tools" or "Voiceover generated with Eleven Labs" in captions (optional, but builds trust)

**Trust Signal:** Being transparent about AI use *increases* credibility with early adopters in productivity/tech space.

## PART 10: WHAT TO AVOID (TikTok Anti-Patterns)

### Algorithm Killers

1. **Slow Intros (0-5s):** Fade-in, logo animation, or talking before the hook. Instant death.
2. **Under-50% Completion Rate:** If 50% of viewers leave before the end, you're shadowbanned until you fix it.
3. **Duplicate Content:** Reposting the same video across multiple accounts (even your own different accounts) gets demoted.
4. **Generic Hooks:** "Hey guys," "This is crazy," "You won't believe this." Overused, ignored by algorithm.
5. **No Clear CTA:** Viewers don't know where to go. No profile clicks. No growth.

### Content Missteps

6. **Switching Niches Too Early:** Post AI productivity, then email management, then calendar hacks, then motivation. Algorithm can't categorize your account. Give content 20-30 videos in one niche before pivoting.
7. **Chasing Trends Instead of Systems:** TikTok rewards consistency. "I'll do viral dances" + core content mixing = lowered reach.
8. **Over-Reliance on Current Followers:** TikTok's follower-first distribution (Jan 2026 update) means first 500-1000 followers are your test audience. But most reach comes from FYP, not followers. Don't assume follower count = reach.
9. **Ignoring Comments:** The algorithm watches whether you engage. Reply to every comment in hour 1.
10. **No Series Content:** Standalone videos get distributed once. Series content gets distributed per episode + binge-watched earlier episodes. Series = 2.8x higher return viewer rate.

### Compliance Missteps

11. **Undisclosed AI Content:** If you're using Eleven Labs voiceover or Synthesia video, label it. Failing to label triggers strikes.
12. **Misleading Thumbnails or Captions:** TikTok penalizes clickbait that doesn't match content ("INSANE RESULTS" then a boring demo).
13. **Green-Screen Spam:** Reposting or low-effort reactions. TikTok's 2025 update specifically penalizes this.
14. **Begging for Shares/Likes in Video:** "Please like and follow" explicitly hurts the algorithm (it's seen as low-intent engagement).

## PART 15: DECISION TREES

### When to Post (If Time Is Limited)

```
Do you have < 2 hours to create?
  YES → Quick Tip format (15-20 sec) or Myth-Buster (20-25 sec)
  NO  → Problem-Solution (25-30 sec) or Series Episode (35-45 sec)

Do you have a performance benchmark for this format already?
  YES → Post that format again (double down on winners)
  NO  → Test a new hook variant (A/B test on same format)

Is it optimal posting time for your account?
  YES → Post immediately, respond to comments for 1 hour
  NO  → Schedule for optimal time (use TikTok Creator Studio)

```

### When to Pivot Format (If Completion Rate Stalls)

```
Completion Rate < 50%?
  YES → Hook problem. Rewrite hook, repost within 3 days
  
Completion Rate 50-65%?
  MAYBE → Check average watch time. 
          If it drops at 8-10s, content is too slow.
          Tighten pacing, repost new version.
          
Completion Rate 65-75%?
  GOOD → Keep format, scale production, vary hook slightly
  
Completion Rate > 75%?
  EXCELLENT → Standardize this format, 60% of future videos
```

### When to Introduce Series Content

```
Do you have 3 videos at 70%+ completion rate?
  YES → You have product-market fit on format. 
        Launch a series (Part 1 of 5, Day 1 of 7, etc.)
  NO  → Keep testing until you do.

Have you hit 500+ followers?
  YES → Series works even better now. Follower-first distribution 
        means your test audience is larger, series completion 
        likely 85%+.
  NO  → Still building critical mass. Series still valuable but 
        growth slower.
```
