<objective>
Retire the legacy local voice pipeline so dAIly has a SINGLE production voice path. Today `src/daily/voice/` (~1,316 LOC: stt.py, tts.py, loop.py, barge_in.py, utils.py) reimplements STT/TTS/barge-in that `src/daily/worker/voice_pipeline.py` already gets for free from the `livekit-agents` library. The two paths use different checkpointers (MemorySaver in the CLI vs AsyncPostgresSaver in the worker), doubling the blast radius of every voice change. This plan proves nothing in production imports `voice/`, deletes it, removes the `daily voice` CLI command, and collapses to AsyncPostgresSaver only.

Purpose: Single-path clarity. Unblocks the E2E smoke test (Plan 05) and Phase 21.5 (Adaptive Learning) on a clean base.
Output: `src/daily/voice/` deleted; `daily voice` CLI command removed; no MemorySaver path; worker/livekit-agents is the sole voice pipeline.
</objective>
