## I. POSTING STRATEGY & CADENCE

### Optimal Schedule
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts per week (4 is sweet spot)
- **Days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (Fri is secondary)
- **Times:** 7-9 AM AND 12-1 PM (audience timezone — use EDT/EST for US)
- **Format Rotation:** 70% carousels, 25% text posts, 5% engagement/questions
- **Consistency:** No gaps > 2 days (algorithm penalizes inactivity)

### 4-3-2-1 Weekly Framework (28-Day Cycle)
| Week Segment | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Posts** | – | Carousel | Text | Carousel | Text |
| **Engagement** | 10-15 comments | 10-15 comments | 10-15 comments | 10-15 comments | 10-15 comments |
| **Format Mix** | – | Educational | Story | Data/Framework | Hot Take |

**Notes:**
- Carousel on Tuesday = highest reach window
- Thursday carousel = catch end-of-week planning scrolling
- Friday text = lighter engagement, used for softer CTAs or personal updates
- No carousel→carousel→carousel sequences (algorithm penalty)
- Daily 15-20 min engagement on other creators' posts (8x algorithmic weight vs likes)

## V. ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

- **Action:** Comment on 10-15 posts from ICP (target founders, operators, VCs)
- **Win condition:** Substantive comments (>15 words) generate 8x algorithmic weight vs generic "Great post!"
- **What to comment on:**

  - Time management discussions

### Connection Building
- **Target:** 10-15 personalized connection requests/week (not mass)
- **Personalization:** Reference their recent post or shared interest
- **Message:** "Saw your post on [topic]. We're building [relevant thing]. Would love to chat."
- **Goal:** Build genuine relationships, not follower count

### First-Hour Engagement Velocity
- **Critical window:** First 60-90 minutes after posting
- **Action:** Reply to EVERY comment within 15 minutes (30% algorithmic boost)

### Saves & Shares (Highest Value Signals)
- **What drives saves:** Frameworks, templates, reusable insights, checklists
- **What drives shares:** Opinions, controversial takes, surprising data
- **Strategy:** Design carousels specifically for "I'll come back to this" behavior (add downloadable template in final slide CTA)

## VI. CONVERSION MECHANICS (Without Links = No Reach Penalty)

### The Problem
External links suppress reach by 60% (LinkedIn algorithm heavily penalizes link-outs).

### The Solution: Multi-Stage Funnel

**Stage 1: Carousel/Text Post (Hook → Interest)**
- No external link in post body
- CTA is comment-based: "Comment WAITLIST and I'll DM you"
- Or: "Save this and DM me if you relate"

**Stage 2: First Comment (Soft CTA)**
- Add first comment yourself immediately after posting
- Include the actual link (waitlist URL, signup page, etc.)
- Format: "For those asking, here's the early access link: [link]"
- Why it works: Link is in comment, not post body (no reach penalty)

**Stage 3: DM Follow-Up**
- When someone comments, reply with a personalized DM
- Example: "Hey [name], thanks for the comment. Here's your early access: [link]. Let me know if you have questions!"
- Goal: Personal touch + direct conversion

### Specific CTAs by Format

**Carousels:**
- "Comment 'BRIEFING' and I'll DM the waitlist"
- "Save this and comment if you want early access"
- "Disagree? Comment your take—let's debate in DMs"

**Text Posts (Lessons/Stories):**
- "What's something you stopped doing that changed your game?" (engagement, not conversion)
- "If this resonates, DM me—early access link in my bio" (bio link is okay; in post is not)

**Hot Takes/Contrarian:**
- "Thoughts? Reply here or DM me—let's chat"

### Profile Optimization for Conversion
1. **Featured Section:** Add a "Join the Waitlist" card/image linking to signup
2. **Bio Link:** Use link-in-bio service (bit.ly, Beacons, etc.) that doesn't count as "external link" in posts
3. **Headline:** "Building dAIly | Voice-first morning briefing | Waitlist opening Q3"

## VII. FORMAT ROTATION PLAN (Prevents Algorithm Penalty)

**Pattern Repetition Killer Rule:** Don't post 3 carousels in a row. Algorithm penalizes format repetition.

### 2-Week Rotation (Optimal)
```
Week 1:
- Mon: –– (no post)
- Tue: Carousel (framework/insights)
- Wed: Text (story/lesson)
- Thu: Carousel (social proof/data)
- Fri: Text (hot take/question)

Week 2:
- Mon: –– (no post)
- Tue: Carousel (new framework)
- Wed: Text (behind-the-scenes)
- Thu: Carousel (customer story)
- Fri: Text (contrarian take)
```

**Variety Signals:**
- Mix carousel topics (don't do "productivity framework" every Tuesday)
- Mix text post types (don't do "lessons" every Wednesday)
- Occasionally swap Thu carousel with Fri text (keeps algorithm guessing)
- One image post per month (breaks pattern subtly)

### Monthly Check-In
- Review which formats got highest engagement
- Rotate the highest-performer to a new day
- Track: Impressions, engagement rate, comments (especially substantive ones)
- Adjust mix based on 4-week rolling average

## VIII. WHAT TO AVOID (LinkedIn Anti-Patterns)

### Format Kills
❌ **Engagement bait:** "Like if you agree," "Comment YES," "Tag someone who..."  
→ LinkedIn algorithm suppresses these explicitly

❌ **Multiple external links in post body:**  
→ 60% reach reduction per link

❌ **Hashtag spam:**  
→ Use 3-5 relevant hashtags max; more looks desperate and lowers reach

❌ **Emojis overuse:**  
→ Looks like legacy 2024 low-effort content. Algorithm penalizes it.

### Content Kills
❌ **Vague motivational content:**  
→ "Success is a journey" — reads as AI filler, suppressed immediately

❌ **Copy-paste templates without personalization:**  
→ LinkedIn detects and lowers distribution

❌ **Duplicate content across multiple posts:**  
→ Algorithm sees as low-effort, reduces reach

❌ **Personal rants without insight:**  
→ "This frustrates me" without "here's why" — doesn't drive engagement

❌ **Hard sell without value delivery:**  
→ 80/20 rule: 80% value, 20% promotion. Reverse this and get shadowbanned

### Engagement Kills
❌ **Generic comments ("Great post!"):**  
→ Zero algorithmic weight, wastes your time

❌ **Replying to comments after 2 hours:**  
→ Miss the algorithmic window; late comments don't boost distribution

❌ **Liking everything indiscriminately:**  
→ LinkedIn tracks engagement authenticity; spam-liking lowers your authority

❌ **Using engagement pods or bot comments:**  
→ 2026 algorithm detects with NLP. Results in shadowban.

### Profile Kills
❌ **Headline that's just your job title:**  
→ "Founder at dAIly" — missed opportunity. Use: "Building dAIly | Voice-first morning briefing for operators"

❌ **Outdated About section:**  
→ Update quarterly. Stale = low authority signal.

❌ **No Featured section:**  
→ Profile visitors can't see your best work. Easy win.
